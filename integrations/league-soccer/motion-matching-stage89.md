# Stage 89 — procedural motion matching

Opt-in replay-only combined profile. Ten immutable procedural gait templates are scored against body-local velocity, braking and turn rate with a prior-profile switch penalty. A selected template determines stride, stance ratio, swing duration, lead and lift. Each newly started swing latches its profile and a predicted world-space landing; corrections are rate limited and cease near landing. Existing recorded contact target, stance anchor, anatomical IK and special-action priorities remain authoritative. The feature does not search motion capture clips, predict tactical intent, modify native positions or ball physics, or invent individual physical traits. Baseline stage 88 remains available. Synthetic tests verify profile selection and invariant match state; audit reports stance rejection, consecutive ankle slip, landings, profile coverage and bone lengths on the same recording. Improvement is not claimed before real comparison.
