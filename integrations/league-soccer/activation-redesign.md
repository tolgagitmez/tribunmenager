# Stage 75 — activation redesign

Historical stage 65, 67 and 68 diagnostics recorded zero activations. This does not establish why. Re-run only one policy per match. The five monotonic counters are inspected (actual pass contact or eligible possession-controller evaluation), context (role/state or first-pass basic context), geometry (position, distance and pressure geometry; triangle first-pass geometry includes skill), available (candidate third player or skill-qualified movement evaluation), started (actual state begins). Counts are controller evaluations, not independent match events. Triangle's inspected count includes active sequences. For duel and hold, a successful state start still requires a safe movement choice and cooldown. Existing success gates remain unchanged.

Triangle: first pass depth 12–58 m, distance 3–23 m, lateral difference >=1 m, receiver passing >=0.45; retain offside, clearance and native second-pass odds.
Duel: ahead 0.35–4.8 m, distance 1.4–5.5 m, closing >=0.25 m/s, ball gap <=1.25 m, skill >=0.48; retain native movement limits, positive score improvement and same-carrier defender-passing evidence.
Hold: depth 9–48 m, forward facing component <=0.45, threat 0.8–4 m, gap <=1.25 m, skill >=0.48, role mindset >=0.45; retain 400 ms controlled hold and actual pass receipt. No new animation or strength physics.

These thresholds are experimental choices, not conclusions from telemetry. No production promotion; successful activation alone is insufficient.
