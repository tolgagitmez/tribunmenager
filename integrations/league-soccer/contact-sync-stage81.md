# Stage 81: accepted native ball-contact synchronization

Experimental trace and replay only. Four accepted native animation touch branches (trap, control, pass, shot) record exact engine clock, player/team identity, selected foot if explicit, pre-touch ball center and requested impulse velocity. The batch is emitted in the next trace frame; no physics, tactics or score changes. Unknown body parts, aerial contacts, goalkeeper and interrupted play do not receive the foot correction. Legacy traces work without events. The opt-in replay uses a bounded +/-100 ms visual cue around the recorded event and existing anatomical IK. This is a toe-target correction, not a full pass/shot clip or verified shoe contact. No contact is inferred from possession or ball velocity; collisions, sliding and keeper actions remain uninstrumented. Diagnostic compares procedural toe distances on identical recordings, excluding unavailable cases, not final WebGL soles. Main game unchanged.
