# Stage 82 — procedural native strike gestures

Separate opt-in replay feature. Native instrumentation adds explicit short-pass, long-pass, high-pass and shot technique codes to accepted low-ball contact records. Old generic pass records use a generic pose, not guessed technique. Motion profiles define preparation, follow-through, foot orientation, torso and counter-arm cues; requested impulse speed scales gesture amplitude, not physical force. The exact recorded contact remains the center of the visual cue. Existing anatomical IK is authoritative. Existing planted support is preserved where possible; no new verified support-foot placement, animation clip, motion capture, foot-surface mesh contact, or full native match equivalence. High passes may be animated when the original ball is low; airborne contacts remain excluded. No simulated positions, ball movement, score or production configuration are changed. Synthetic unit tests and WebGL smoke are not evidence of visual realism.
