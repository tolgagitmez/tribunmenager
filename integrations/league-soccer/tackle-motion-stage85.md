# Stage 85 — tackle and recovery presentation

Opt-in replay only. Accepted native Interfere and Sliding ball touches now have separate event types. Actual referee AddFoul calls are captured independently with offender and victim identity, clock and tackle type; no foul is inferred from contact alone. The contacting foot receives a low-ball standing or sliding gesture; sliding lowers the pelvis. Recorded higher-severity trip events can drive a bounded crouch and recovery over up to 900 ms, not a complete ground fall or get-up clip. Existing anatomical solver remains authoritative. Unknown feet, high balls and interrupted replay are excluded. No new ball-winning AI, verified steal outcome, exact slide ground contact, collision solver, card decision or production change. Foul records are audited, not yet rendered as a referee signal.
