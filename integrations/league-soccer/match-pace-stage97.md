# Stage 97 — five-minute match pace

Isolated experimental flag TRIBUN_NATIVE_MATCH_PACE. Adds a safe progressive short-pass candidate and bounded tempo-sensitive bonus in the native ranking, only in open play with controlled possession and offside margin. No backpass ban, forced pass, altered ball velocity or global shot threshold. Adds close-range good-angle quality-guarded shot priority; uses existing native shot decision and physics. Enables existing shorter ordinary ball-out restart preparation (throw-in, corner, goal kick only), excluding foul, goal and halftime; fixed 300s clock unchanged. Collective profile is not combined until separately qualified. Paired benchmark: six matches with same fixture/seed/runtime, evaluates shots, on-target, controlled box entries, pass accuracy, open-play and dead-ball time. Gates require more shots and box entries in aggregate, no aggregate on-target/open-play loss, per-fixture pass accuracy drop <=6 percentage points and delivery. Outlet evaluation counter is not actual pass count. Effects of the three changes are not individually isolated; no production promotion or proven improvement.
