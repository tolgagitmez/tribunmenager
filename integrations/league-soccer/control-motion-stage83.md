# Stage 83 — recorded control and dribbling presentation

Separate opt-in replay mode. Actual accepted trap/control events gain incoming ball velocity, player velocity/facing, nearest opponent distance, effective dribbling/ball-control values, knock-on modifier and possession state. The replay chooses procedural cushion, close-control, running-control, turn-control or knock-on motion from these facts; the direction-based turn profile is a visual classification, not proof of a feint or beaten opponent. The recorded contacting foot remains authoritative. Skill changes the visual excursion; pressure plus confirmed possession changes body/arm posture. The foot angle is stylistic and does not identify the real boot surface. No new AI dribble action, forced foot alternation, ball impulse, simulated position, score, motion capture, or production behavior. Legacy records receive a generic control/trap gesture without invented pressure/skill context. The audit counts eligible and presented events and anatomical errors, not visual quality or actual dribble success.
